Bright TV Dashboard Case Study

## 1. Overview of the Case Study

This case study focuses on analyzing the **Bright TV dataset** by developing an interactive dashboard using **Google Looker Studio (Data Studio)**. The purpose of the dashboard is to provide meaningful insights into TV viewership by examining viewer behaviour, channel performance, peak viewing times, and audience demographics. The dashboard enables stakeholders to make informed decisions by presenting data through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs). To ensure consistency, the same visualizations were also recreated in **Microsoft Excel** using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI cards, and slicers.

## 2. How the Case Study Was Completed and Insights Found

The project began with reviewing and understanding the Bright TV dataset. The data was cleaned and organized to ensure accurate reporting and analysis. A dashboard plan was created to determine the layout, KPIs, charts, and filters before developing the final dashboard in Looker Studio.

The dashboard was divided into five sections:

* **Dashboard Overview** – Displays KPI cards showing Total Viewing Records, Total Viewing Hours, Average Watch Duration, Number of TV Channels, and the Top Performing TV Channel, along with monthly viewership trends.
* **Channel Performance** – Compares TV channels based on total views, watch time, and average viewing duration.
* **Viewer Behaviour** – Analyzes viewing patterns by hour of the day, day of the week, weekday versus weekend viewing, and peak viewing periods.
* **Audience Segments** – Examines viewership by region, age group, gender, and social media or email flag (where applicable).
* **Insights and Recommendations** – Summarizes the key findings and provides recommendations for improving viewership and content performance.


### Key Insights

* Certain TV channels consistently attract the highest number of viewers.
* Peak viewing occurs during specific hours of the day, making them ideal for premium content and advertising.
* Weekend viewership is generally higher than weekday viewership.
* Some regions and age groups contribute more significantly to overall viewing activity.
* Average watch duration helps identify channels that retain viewers for longer periods.

---

## 3. Summary of What Was Done and What Was Found

An interactive dashboard was successfully created to analyze the Bright TV dataset. The dashboard provides a clear overview of viewing trends, channel performance, audience behaviour, and customer segments. Interactive filters allow users to explore the data by month, channel, region, age group, and gender.

The analysis identified the highest-performing TV channels, peak viewing periods, audience demographics, and regional viewing trends. These insights can support better programming decisions, improve marketing strategies, optimize advertising schedules, and increase audience engagement.

---

## 4. Tools Used

The following tools were used to complete the case study:

* **Google Looker Studio (Data Studio)** – To build the interactive dashboard and visualize the Bright TV dataset.
* **Microsoft Excel** – To recreate the dashboard using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI cards, slicers, and conditional formatting.
* **Google Sheets / Microsoft Excel** – For organizing, preparing, and validating the dataset before visualization.

---

## Conclusion

The Bright TV Dashboard successfully transforms raw viewership data into meaningful business insights. By combining interactive dashboards with Excel-based reporting, the project provides a comprehensive understanding of viewer behaviour, channel performance, audience segments, and peak viewing times. These insights can help stakeholders optimize programming schedules, improve audience targeting, and make data-driven decisions to enhance overall TV viewership.
